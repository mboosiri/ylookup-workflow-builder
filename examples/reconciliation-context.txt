The customer imports the bank statement, identifies each payment using full names
where possible and partial names where necessary, and leaves uncertain matches
for review. They record what each investor paid against what was called. Finally,
they build the accounting upload: two balancing lines, cash received and investor
receivable reduced, correct account codes from the chart, linked entries and totals
that reconcile. The cash debit increases cash by the actual payment; the matching
receivable credit has the same amount. Zero receipts apply to non-payers, not to
the cash side of a paid investor. Infer the demonstrated output conventions from the completed file;
ask when essential business information is ambiguous.
