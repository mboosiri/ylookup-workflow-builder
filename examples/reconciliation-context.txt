First, I pull the bank statement into a sheet. Then, I figure out who each payment is from. The bank references are a mess — sometimes the name is truncated or split up. I match using full names if possible, or partial names if needed. If I can't match it, I leave it blank to chase up later.

Next, I log what they paid versus what they owe. Finally, I build the upload file. In accounting, every transaction requires two balancing lines: one showing cash came in, and one showing the investor owes us less. I look up the correct account codes, link the lines, and ensure the totals tie out.
